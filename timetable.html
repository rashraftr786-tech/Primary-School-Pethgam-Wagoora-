📅 Timetable Version

This timetable is prepared for a specified effective period. It does not require separate entries for individual days. When the timetable is changed after three or four months, create a new version and keep the previous version in history.
Example: 01/09/2026 → 30/11/2026. The timetable remains applicable throughout this effective period until a new version is prepared.

⏰ Time Structure

General gaps can be things such as Morning Assembly, Lunch Break, Recess Break, Prayer, Activity, or any other school-wide gap.

👩‍🏫 Teaching Assignments

Import active teachers and their class-wise subject assignments from Staff Management. Example: 5th — English, 4th — Mathematics. The imported assignments control the class/subject choices available in each teacher's timetable periods.

➕ General Gaps / Breaks

The number of general gaps is adjustable. Each gap can have its own name, duration and position.
